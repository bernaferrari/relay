title: Connect an iPhone or iPad
description: Prepare an iOS simulator or physical device, verify controls, and record a qualified flow.

## Prepare the Mac

iOS testing requires **macOS and Xcode**. Open Xcode and finish its initial setup. Start a simulator, or connect a trusted physical iPhone or iPad.

Physical devices may require Developer Mode and the relevant device services. Follow the device's and Xcode's prompts, then check prerequisites:

```bash
pnpm doctor
```

## Keep the device ready

Open the app you intend to test. Keep the device unlocked, awake, and reserved for the recording or Run. On iPad, check that keyboard, window, or multitasking state has not moved the target out of the expected layout.

## Verify pixels and controls

Open **Devices** and select the exact device. Check its live preview and inspectable controls.

Pixels and accessibility are separate capabilities. Relay may be able to display the screen while its accessibility session cannot return controls. If that happens, keep the app open, choose **Reconnect** once, and check again.

Do not infer tap coordinates from screenshot pixel dimensions: Retina captures and logical input coordinates can differ. Relay uses the current app window's logical bounds for supervised preview input.

## Record and replay

[Record a short journey](/docs/workflows/recording), save it, and [run it again](/docs/workflows/running) on the same device and configuration. Choose whether to restart the app or begin from its current state.

A control-specific capture belongs to that recorded step, app, and device profile. If it no longer matches, record the affected step again from the correct screen.

## Current limitations

Native capture and control support varies by device, OS, app, and build. Full-flow video is not qualified on every physical iOS path; lightweight screenshots and structure are the default. Capture saving can also be slower than browser execution.

Avoid competing automation clients during qualification. If a connection is interrupted, use the [recovery steps](/docs/reference/troubleshooting) before resuming.
